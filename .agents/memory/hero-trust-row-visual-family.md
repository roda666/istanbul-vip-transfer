---
name: Hero trust-row visual family
description: Owner-approved visual rule for trust and metric rows on the cream homepage hero.
---

Homepage hero trust statistics shown directly on the cream background must remain unboxed and use the same gold serif value plus small gray uppercase label treatment as the existing airport/support/vehicle trust row. Use subtle separators for structure rather than a dark filled card.

**Why:** The owner confirmed that a dark navy metric card felt visually disconnected and too heavy on the flat cream hero; that treatment is appropriate only when overlaid on a dark photo.

**How to apply:** Keep both hero trust rows in one visual family. On narrow screens, a 2×2 metric grid may use restrained internal horizontal and vertical separators while preserving the open background.
