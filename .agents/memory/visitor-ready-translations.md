---
name: Visitor-ready translation policy
description: Public visibility rule for completed service and blog translations.
---

Completed localized content may be served to visitors while its administrative workflow status is DRAFT, REVIEW, or APPROVED, as well as when it is PUBLISHED or OUTDATED. Rows still being translated or that failed must remain hidden.

**Why:** The product requirement is that international visitors receive their own complete language immediately, with no Turkish fallback or review-state warning.

**How to apply:** Keep public service/blog readers and locale availability checks aligned with this visibility set. Administrative controls may still track approval and publishing independently.
