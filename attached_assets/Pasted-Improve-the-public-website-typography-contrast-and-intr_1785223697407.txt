Improve the public website typography contrast and introduce a refined blue-led color identity.

PROBLEM

Text on white and light backgrounds currently looks washed out because gray colors are too light and some text uses reduced opacity.

REQUIREMENTS

1. Replace low-contrast gray text across all public pages.

Use:
- Headings: #102A43
- Body text: #263F55
- Secondary text: #50677A
- Placeholder text: #667C8E
- Links: #1D5FD1
- Links hover: #174EA6

2. Do not use opacity to create muted body text.
Avoid:
- opacity below 1 on paragraphs
- text-gray-300
- text-gray-400 on light backgrounds
- pale blue-gray text that is difficult to read

3. Normal paragraph text must meet WCAG AA contrast.
Long-form blog and service text should be especially comfortable to read.

4. Introduce blue as the primary modern accent:
- Primary blue: #2563EB
- Dark blue: #173B67
- Blue hover: #1D4ED8

Use blue for:
- Links
- Focus rings
- Selected controls
- Secondary CTA buttons
- Small icons
- Form focus states

5. Preserve premium gold #C99A32 for:
- Logo
- Important highlights
- Decorative lines
- Premium labels
- Selected heading words

6. Purple may be used only as a limited optional accent:
- Purple: #6D4BC3
- Use only for small badges, subtle icon details or very restrained blue-to-purple gradients.
- Do not use purple for large page backgrounds.
- Do not combine blue, purple and gold heavily in the same component.

7. Keep page backgrounds eye-friendly:
- Main: #F7F8FC
- Warm/light surface: #FFFDF8
- Alternate: #EEF3F9
- Cards: #FFFFFF

8. Audit all public pages and shared components, not only the homepage.

9. Verify actual rendered contrast on:
- Homepage
- Service pages
- Vehicles
- Blog
- Contact
- Booking form
- FAQ
- Footer

10. Preserve all content, routes, SEO, forms, images and functionality.

11. Do not modify the admin panel, credentials, Secrets or database.

12. Run lint, typecheck and production build.

13. Do not publish, deploy or connect the production domain.
